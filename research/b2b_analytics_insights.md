# B2B Analytics Research Findings

**Source:** 180ops B2B Customer Insights Guide  
**Date:** November 1, 2025

## Key B2B Analytics Principles

### 1. Account-Based Customer View
- Unify all data sources into single account view
- Include contact activity, sales history, engagement, financial status
- Real-time updates as customer behavior changes
- Enables cross-department collaboration

### 2. Revenue Opportunity Identification
- Cross-sell chances
- Upsell readiness
- New sales potential
- Churn risk detection

### 3. Predictive Insights
- AI/ML to study buying patterns
- Engagement signal analysis
- Sales forecasting
- Churn risk prediction
- Buying readiness scoring

### 4. Actionable Recommendations
- Next best action for each account
- Real-time alerts based on signals
- Data-backed suggestions for sales teams

### 5. Cross-Department Alignment
- Shared KPIs across sales, marketing, finance
- Common language and metrics
- Unified customer view for all teams

## Key Metrics for B2B Food Service

### Customer Retention Metrics
- Customer lifetime value (CLV)
- Churn rate
- Repeat purchase rate
- Purchase frequency
- Average order value (AOV)

### Usage Tracking
- Product usage by customer
- Category-level consumption
- Seasonal trends
- Volume changes over time

### Opportunity Detection
- Products customer stopped buying
- Products customer never bought but should
- Cross-sell opportunities (cup → lid)
- Upsell opportunities (volume discounts)

### Pricing Analytics
- Price sensitivity analysis
- Competitive pricing insights
- Predicted win rates at different price points
- Customer-specific pricing optimization

## Application to B2B+ Platform

### Historical Data Integration
- Import 2023-2024 Bailey usage data
- Import 2025 brokerage sales data
- Map SKUs across systems using AI
- Create unified customer purchase history

### AI-Powered Analytics
- Usage forecasting by product/category
- Customer behavior pattern detection
- Opportunity scoring (what they stopped buying)
- Pricing optimization recommendations

### Customer Retention Features
- "You used to buy this" alerts
- Product recommendation engine
- Frequently bought together
- Category-based cross-sell suggestions

### Admin Dashboard Enhancements
- Customer usage trends
- Product performance analytics
- Opportunity pipeline
