# B2B Plus AI Features - User Guide

## Overview

B2B Plus includes powerful AI features powered by Google Gemini to help you manage your business more effectively. This guide covers all AI features available to customers and administrators.

## For Customers

### AI Chatbot Assistant

The AI chatbot helps you quickly find information and complete tasks without navigating through multiple pages.

#### How to Access
1. Click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner of any page
2. Or navigate to the Chat page from the main menu

#### What the Chatbot Can Do

**Check Order Status**
- Ask: "What's the status of my recent orders?"
- Ask: "Where is order #12345?"
- The chatbot will fetch your order details and provide current status

**Search Products**
- Ask: "Show me products in the Electronics category"
- Ask: "Find products under $100"
- Get instant product recommendations

**View Cart**
- Ask: "What's in my cart?"
- Get a summary of your current cart items and total

**Track Shipments**
- Ask: "Track my shipment"
- Get tracking information for your orders

**Answer Questions**
- Ask general questions about products, pricing, or policies
- Get instant answers powered by AI

#### Tips for Best Results
- Be specific in your questions
- Use natural language - no need for special commands
- If the chatbot doesn't understand, try rephrasing your question
- The chatbot has access to your order history and account information

### Public Chatbot (For Visitors)

If you're not logged in, you can still chat with our AI assistant:

1. Visit the homepage
2. Click the chat widget
3. Ask questions about our products and services
4. Provide your contact information to receive personalized assistance

## For Administrators

### AI Opportunity Detection

The AI automatically analyzes customer behavior to identify sales opportunities.

#### Types of Opportunities

**Stopped Buying Detection**
- Identifies customers who stopped purchasing products they used to buy regularly
- Shows last purchase date and frequency
- Provides AI-generated insights on why they might have stopped

**Cross-Sell Opportunities**
- Suggests complementary products based on customer purchase history
- Identifies products that pair well with what customers already buy

**Upsell Opportunities**
- Recommends premium or higher-value alternatives
- Shows potential revenue increase

#### How to Use

1. Navigate to **Admin → Opportunities**
2. View the list of AI-detected opportunities
3. Filter by type (Stopped Buying, Cross-Sell, Upsell)
4. Filter by status (Open, Pursued, Dismissed)
5. Click an opportunity to see details and AI reasoning
6. Click "Pursue" to take action on an opportunity
7. Create tasks or contact customers directly

#### Opportunity Scores

Each opportunity has a score (0-100) indicating:
- Likelihood of success
- Potential revenue impact
- Customer engagement level

Focus on high-scoring opportunities first for best results.

### AI Pricing Optimization

Get AI-powered pricing recommendations to maximize revenue and win rates.

#### How It Works

The AI analyzes:
- Customer purchase history
- Historical pricing data
- Win/loss patterns
- Market conditions
- Competitor pricing (if available)

#### How to Use

1. Navigate to **Admin → Pricing**
2. View pricing recommendations
3. Each recommendation shows:
   - Current price
   - Suggested price
   - Win probability
   - Expected revenue impact
   - AI reasoning

4. Review the AI's analysis
5. Click "Approve" to apply the new price
6. Click "Reject" to dismiss the recommendation

#### Best Practices

- Review AI reasoning before approving
- Consider customer relationships and history
- Monitor win rates after price changes
- Use confidence levels to prioritize reviews

### AI Invoice Processing

Automatically extract data from vendor invoices using AI.

#### How to Use

1. Navigate to **Admin → Invoices**
2. Click "Upload Invoice"
3. Select a PDF invoice file
4. The AI will automatically extract:
   - Invoice number
   - Vendor name
   - Invoice date
   - Total amount
   - Line items with quantities and prices

5. Review the extracted data
6. Make any necessary corrections
7. Save to your system

#### Supported Formats

- PDF invoices
- Scanned documents
- Digital invoices
- Multi-page documents

#### Tips

- Ensure invoices are clear and readable
- The AI works best with standard invoice formats
- Review extracted data before saving
- Report any extraction errors for improvement

### AI Monitoring Dashboard

Track AI performance and usage across your organization.

#### How to Access

Navigate to **Admin → Monitoring**

#### Metrics Available

**Usage Metrics**
- Total AI requests
- Requests by type (chatbot, opportunities, pricing, invoices)
- Token usage
- Cache hit rates

**Performance Metrics**
- Average response times
- Error rates
- Throughput (requests per minute)
- Performance trends over time

**Business Metrics**
- Opportunities pursued
- Pricing approvals
- Leads captured
- Revenue impact

#### Using the Dashboard

1. **Summary Cards** - Quick overview of key metrics
2. **Usage by Type** - Detailed breakdown of AI usage
3. **Business Metrics** - Track business outcomes
4. **Performance Trends** - Monitor system health
5. **Recent Activity** - See real-time AI operations

#### Performance Targets

- **Response Time**: < 2 seconds
- **Cache Hit Rate**: > 50%
- **Error Rate**: < 5%

If metrics fall outside these ranges, contact support.

## Privacy and Security

### Data Protection

- All AI operations are isolated by organization
- You can only access your organization's data
- AI responses never include data from other organizations
- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest

### Rate Limiting

To ensure fair usage and system stability:
- Chatbot: 10 requests per minute per user
- Admin AI features: 5 requests per minute per user
- Rate limits reset every minute

If you hit a rate limit, wait a moment and try again.

### Data Retention

- Chat conversations: Stored for 90 days
- AI metrics: Stored for 1 year
- Business metrics: Stored indefinitely

## Troubleshooting

### Chatbot Not Responding

1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try logging out and back in

### AI Errors

If you see an error message:
1. Note the error message
2. Try the operation again
3. If it persists, contact support with:
   - What you were trying to do
   - The error message
   - Time of the error

### Slow Response Times

- Check the monitoring dashboard for system status
- Peak usage times may have slower responses
- Cached responses are faster - repeated queries will be quicker
